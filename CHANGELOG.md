# Changelog

## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-02

- ALL THE THINGS' quests in EasyFind's search, found by name, questline or reward; the line under each one's name says where it is, and its tooltip whether you have done it. Click for directions, Alt-click to open it in ALL THE THINGS. With the Questie extension on, its info goes onto the quests Questie shows instead.
- On EasyFind's own mount, pet, toy, appearance, item, heirloom, title, achievement, recipe and decor rows, the line under the name says where the thing comes from.
- Hover one: its tooltip says whether you have it and lists each place it comes from, in ALL THE THINGS' words.
- Right-click it for Open in ALL THE THINGS.
- A switch per kind in the filter menu turns its info off on those rows, never the rows themselves; its own quest rows also have Hide completed and Near me.
- `@att` searches only what ALL THE THINGS knows; alone, it lists what is left to get on the map you are on. The ALL THE THINGS button opens it.
- Retail, WoW Forever, Mists Classic, TBC Anniversary and Classic Era.
